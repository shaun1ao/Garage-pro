# 🚗 Garage Pro

> **Every bay. Every job. Under control.**

Garage Pro is a modern, cloud-powered garage management platform built for independent workshops and multi-branch service centres.

It replaces paper job cards, spreadsheets and messaging apps with one integrated system for bookings, job tracking, invoicing, inventory management and workshop operations.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 Workshop Management
- Live multi-bay dashboard
- Digital job cards
- Vehicle check-in & check-out
- Walk-in customer support
- Bay allocation and queue management
- Multiple mechanics per job

### 👥 Customer Management
- Customer database
- Complete vehicle service history
- Fast customer lookup
- Automatic customer information recall

### 📦 Inventory
- Real-time inventory tracking
- Automatic stock deduction
- Low-stock alerts
- Parts usage tracking

### 🧾 Invoicing
- GST-ready PDF invoices
- HSN/SAC support
- Discounts & payment tracking
- Email invoices directly to customers

### ☁️ Cloud Sync
- Real-time cloud synchronisation
- Offline-first operation
- Automatic data syncing
- Multi-device support

### 🔐 User Management
- Role-based permissions
- Custom user roles
- Worker PIN login
- Secure workshop accounts
- Audit logging

### 📊 Reports
- Revenue reports
- Mechanic performance
- Inventory reports
- Daily workshop summaries
- Export to CSV

### 📸 Job Documentation
- Before & after photos
- PDF inspection reports
- Insurance documentation
