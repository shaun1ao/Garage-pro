The platform

One system for the whole workshop

Six things every garage juggles across notebooks, spreadsheets and chat groups — brought into a single, real-time app.

Live floor & job cards

Every bay at a glance — free, in service, ready for collection — with digital job cards carrying itemised parts, labour and multi-mechanic assignment. Move a car across the floor and everyone sees it instantly.

  • Multi-bay status board
  • Digital vehicle inspection
  • Tap-to-mark damage diagram

GST invoicing & payments

Proper GST invoices with HSN/SAC codes, discount breakdown and amount in words — numbered sequentially so nothing duplicates. Take partial payments, chase the outstanding balance, mark paid in one tap.

  • 18% GST & HSN/SAC built in
  • Partial payments & balance chasing
  • Print or share as a link

Parts & inventory

A dedicated request workflow — workers ask, parts prices and orders, everyone tracks status. Stock deducts automatically as parts are billed, and open estimates reserve stock so you never quote a part twice.

  • Automatic stock deduction
  • Reserved-stock awareness
  • Barcode & QR scanning

Customer experience

Customers approve estimates line by line on their own phone, watch their car's live status on a Track Repair page, and hear from you over WhatsApp and email — right up to the thank-you with the invoice attached.

  • Tap-to-approve estimates
  • Live "Track Repair" page
  • WhatsApp & email updates

Your team, in control

Seven built-in roles plus custom ones, each seeing only the tabs they should. Workers sign in with a personal PIN on a shared shop-floor device, clock hours for payroll, and every action lands in a searchable audit log.

  • Role-based access & worker PINs
  • Timesheets for payroll
  • Full audit trail

Reports & multi-branch

Revenue trends, turnaround times, inventory usage and per-mechanic performance over any date range — with a morning briefing and end-of-day summary by email. Run a chain? Roll every branch up into one dashboard.

  • Revenue & performance analytics
  • Daily briefing & closing summary
  • Chain HQ roll-up

How it works

From booking to paid in three steps

The same flow your garage already follows — just faster, tidier and without the paperwork.

1

Book

Take the booking, pick the bay and assign a mechanic. Customer and vehicle details auto-fill from history.

2

Service

Mechanics log parts and labour on the job card from any device. Stock deducts automatically as work gets done.

3

Invoice

Generate a GST-ready PDF in one tap, email it to the customer and mark the job done. Money in, bay free.
