Features

Everything your workshop runs on — in one app

Stop juggling notebooks, spreadsheets and chat groups. Garage Pro keeps the whole floor, every job and every rupee in one place.

Multi-bay live status

See every bay at a glance — free, in service or ready for collection — and move jobs across the floor in real time.

Job cards & parts tracking

Full digital job cards with itemised parts, labour and multi-mechanic assignment — nothing falls through the cracks.

Professional GST invoices

PDF invoices with GST, HSN/SAC codes, discount breakdown and amount in words — print or email in one tap.

Real-time cloud sync

Firebase-powered sync keeps the front desk, workshop and your phone perfectly in step across every device.

Tablet web interface

Mechanics update jobs from any browser on the network — no install needed. Grease-friendly, tap-first screens.

Customer & vehicle history

Every customer, every vehicle, every past service in one searchable database — recall a car's full history instantly.

Inventory & auto stock

Track parts in real time with automatic stock deduction as they're billed — never oversell a part again.

CSV import

Bring your existing customers, inventory and bookings across in minutes with simple CSV import — same day switch.

Email notifications

Auto emails when a job starts, a car's ready for collection or an invoice is attached — customers stay in the loop.

Daily morning briefing

A summary email lands in the manager's inbox each morning — bookings, pending jobs and yesterday's numbers.

Offline-first & private

Data stored locally outside any cloud sync folder — Garage Pro keeps working even when the internet doesn't.

How it works

From booking to paid in three steps

The same flow your garage already follows — just faster, tidier and without the paperwork.

1

Book

Take the booking, pick the bay and assign a mechanic. Customer and vehicle details auto-fill from history.

2

Service

Mechanics log parts and labour on the job card from a tablet. Stock deducts automatically as work gets done.

3

Invoice

Generate a GST-ready PDF in one tap, email it to the customer and mark the job done. Money in, bay free.
