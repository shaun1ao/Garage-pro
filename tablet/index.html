Open your workshop tablet from anywhere

1

2
Pick the worker (or admin) on the PIN pad — same PINs your team already uses on the in-shop tablets.
3
Update jobs, check vehicles in/out, scan barcodes, clock in/out — all live-synced with the desktop app.
