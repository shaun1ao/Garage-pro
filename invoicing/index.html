Invoicing

GST Invoicing Software for Indian Workshops

Proper GST invoices, generated straight from the job card — no re-typing parts and labour into a separate billing tool.

Start free — no credit card required Read the docs

GST & HSN/SAC built in

Every invoice carries proper GST at 18% with HSN/SAC codes, a discount breakdown, and the total spelled out in words — the format a workshop actually needs to hand a customer or file against.

  • 18% GST calculated automatically per line
  • HSN/SAC codes on every part and service
  • Amount in words printed on the invoice
  • Sequential invoice numbering — nothing duplicates

Partial payments & balance chasing

Take a partial payment against a job without losing track of what's still owed — the outstanding balance stays visible, and following up on it is part of the same workflow, not a separate spreadsheet.

  • Partial payments recorded against any invoice
  • Outstanding balance always visible, per customer
  • Mark paid in one tap once settled

Print, share or link

Hand over a printed copy at the counter, or share the invoice as a link over WhatsApp or email — the customer sees the same GST-ready invoice either way.

  • Print-ready layout
  • Shareable link — WhatsApp or email, no attachment needed

Connected to job cards & inventory

An invoice is generated directly from the job card's approved estimate — parts billed on it deduct from inventory automatically, so stock and billing never drift apart.

  • No re-entry — approved job card lines carry straight through
  • Automatic stock deduction when parts are billed
