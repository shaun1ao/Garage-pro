Inventory

Garage Inventory Management Made Simple

Know what's on the shelf, what's already spoken for, and what's about to run out — without a separate spreadsheet to keep updating.

Start free — no credit card required Read the docs

Parts requests, priced and tracked

A worker on the floor raises a parts request against a job card. It gets priced, ordered and its status tracked — everyone from the mechanic to the front desk sees exactly where a part stands, instead of chasing it over calls.

  • Dedicated request workflow — raise, price and approve parts per job
  • Status visible to everyone — no more "did the part come in?" calls
  • Cost & sell price tracked separately — margins stay accurate

Automatic stock deduction

Bill a part on an invoice and it comes off inventory automatically — no separate step to remember, no manual recount at month-end to catch what slipped through.

  • Deducts on billing, not on manual entry
  • Job-card deletion is safe by default — deleting a job card only cleans up the page; parts only return to stock if you explicitly tick the refund checkbox

Reserved-stock awareness

An open estimate that's already using a part reserves that stock — so you never quote the same last unit to two different customers at once.

  • Open estimates reserve stock automatically
  • Low-stock alerts — know before you're caught short mid-job

Barcode & QR scanning

Scan a part in or out instead of typing part numbers by hand — quicker at the counter, and fewer typos in the stock list.

  • Barcode & QR scanning built into the app, on any device
