Cookie Policy

What we store on your device, why, which third parties are involved, and how to change your mind at any time.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1.Your choice

We use only the storage the site needs to work. Analytics is off unless you switch it on. “Reject” and “Accept” are equally easy, you can use the whole site either way, and you can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal we treat it as “no” to analytics.

2.Strictly necessary (always on)

These cannot be switched off because the site or app would not work without them. They are not used for advertising or tracking.

NamePurposeLasts
gp_consent_v1Remembers your cookie choiceUntil you clear it
gp-themeYour light/dark choiceUntil you clear it
gp_lang (cookie and local storage), gp_lang_okYour language choice, and that you agreed to translated pages inside the app1 year
gp_contact_popup_dismissedRemembers that you closed the contact pop-up so it doesn’t come backUntil you clear it
Firebase sign-in (IndexedDB / firebase:authUser…)Keeps you signed in to your account and the appUntil you sign out
gp_book_meOnly if you tick “Remember my details” on a booking page: your name, phone and car so you don’t retype themUntil you clear it

3.Analytics (only if you accept)

If you accept analytics we use Google Analytics, configured with advertising features and Google signals off, and our own simple visit counter, to learn which pages people read and roughly where visitors come from. Nothing in the workshop app’s customer data is part of this.

NamePurposeWhoLasts
_ga, _ga_*Counts visits and pagesGoogleUp to 2 years
gp_vid (local storage), gp_sid (session)Tells a returning visitor from a new one in our own counterUsUntil cleared / the tab closes
Coarse location lookupCountry and city for the visitor map. Your IP address is sent to a location service (ipapi.co, or geojs.io as a backup) to get this; we do not store the IP addressipapi.co / geojs.ioOne request per visit

If you decline, or later withdraw, none of the above is loaded or kept, and we delete the Google Analytics cookies and our visitor ID from your browser.

4.Payments

When you start a checkout, the page loads Lemon Squeezy’s payment overlay (it is not loaded before that). Lemon Squeezy is our merchant of record and sets its own cookies to run and secure the payment; see its policy for details. We never see or store your card details.

5.Pages your customers see

Booking, tracking, estimate and similar pages that a workshop sends to its customers set no tracking or advertising cookies and load no analytics. They use only the necessary storage above, and fonts are served from our own site, not from Google.

6.Third-party services we use

  • Google Firebase — account sign-in and the database that holds your workshop’s data (necessary).
  • Lemon Squeezy — payments for subscriptions, only when you check out.
  • Cloudinary — stores and serves photos workshops upload to job cards.
  • Resend and Vercel — send the emails your workshop’s customers and you receive.
  • Cloudflare — runs the small service that activates a subscription after payment.
  • Google Analytics and the location lookup above — only with your consent.
  • We removed Google Fonts: the fonts are now hosted on our own site, so your browser does not contact Google to display text.

We review this list whenever we add or change a service, and we load nothing from a third party that this page does not mention.
