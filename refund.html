Refund Policy

Plain terms on refunds and cancelling. Prices are shown in full before you pay; there are no hidden fees.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1.The short version

  • You can cancel any time, in two clicks, and you will not be charged again.
  • We will refund your latest payment if you ask within 14 days of buying or renewing and have used Garage Pro for no more than 1 hour in total since that purchase or renewal.
  • If you have used it for more than 1 hour since you bought or renewed, we do not give refunds for that period — but you keep full access until it ends, and you are never charged again after cancelling.
  • Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by anything on this page.

2.How “1 hour of use” is measured

Garage Pro records the time the web app is open and actively used (clicks, typing, navigating) for each paid period. Idle time while the app is open in the background is not counted. The count starts again from zero at every purchase and every renewal, so a refund request is always judged on the period you are asking about. We can show you the figure for your account on request.

At the moment, only use of the web app is counted; time in the mobile app is not, so it cannot count against you.

3.How to ask for a refund

Email support@garagepro.me from the address you signed up with, and say which payment it is about. We reply within 3 working days. If you qualify, the refund goes back to the original payment method through our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, and normally reaches you in 5–10 working days (your bank may take longer to show it).

4.How to cancel

  • In the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage or cancel, then press Cancel subscription in the billing page that opens. No call, no email, no questions.
  • Or use the link in any Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or email support@garagepro.me and we will cancel it for you the same day.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

5.No hidden fees

The price you see on the pricing page is the price you pay, plus any sales tax, VAT or GST that applies in your country — shown at checkout before you confirm. There are no setup, cancellation, early-termination or “processing” fees. Renewals are at the same price and billing cycle you chose, and Lemon Squeezy emails you a receipt for every payment.

6.Other cases

  • Charged twice or by mistake: tell us and we will refund the duplicate in full, whatever the usage.
  • A fault we cannot fix: if Garage Pro does not work as described and we cannot put it right, we will refund the current period even if you used it for more than 1 hour.
  • Plan changes: upgrading or downgrading starts a new billing period, and this policy applies to that new payment.
  • Chargebacks: please contact us first — we would rather fix it quickly than have you wait on your bank.

7.Your statutory rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights that this policy cannot reduce (for example a cooling-off period for online purchases, or rights if a service is faulty). Nothing here limits them. If a law gives you a better refund than this policy, the law applies.
